# Worked Example: Coffee Route

Input: “一杯早晨喝到的咖啡，背后是一条从山坡到烘焙机，再到城市吧台的接力。”

## Meaning map

- Claim: an ordinary cup depends on several linked transformations and transfers.
- Starting state: ripe coffee cherries on a highland branch.
- Change engine: harvesting, processing, transport, roasting, and brewing.
- Ending state: a finished cup at a city counter.
- Concrete inventory: branch, cherries, drying trays, sack, ship or truck fragment, roasting drum, beans, grinder, cup.
- Forbidden readings: coffee appears instantly; unrelated travel montage; luxury-product advertisement.

## Architecture

Evidence chain, 15 seconds, four states:

1. cherries are picked into a printed sack;
2. dried beans travel as the sack becomes a shipping parcel;
3. beans enter a roasting drum and darken;
4. ground coffee passes through a filter and fills a cup.

Continuity token: a red diagonal stripe with one circular punch. It appears on the sack, parcel label, roasting-drum marker, filter tab, and cup sleeve.

## Visual proof

First glance: bright red cherries and the striped collection sack establish the source. Tracked change: the striped token accompanies the material while cherries become pale beans, shipped beans, roasted beans, and grounds. Concluding evidence: the same stripe locks around the finished cup while small earlier states align behind it as a connected path.

## Motion priorities

- Meaningful motion begins immediately with the first cherry dropping.
- Each transfer uses a physical paper mechanism: fold, stitch, slide, rotate on a fastener, crush, and filter.
- One dominant action occurs at a time; secondary beans bridge the cuts.
- The finished cup settles during only the final 0.35 seconds.

This example demonstrates the method. Do not reuse its objects, token, palette, or composition as a default template.
